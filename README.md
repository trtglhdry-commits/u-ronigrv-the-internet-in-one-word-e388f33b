# The Internet in One Word — enshittification

[![make.fun rank](https://make.fun/badge/the-internet-in-one-word/trtglhdry-commits/u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/c/the-internet-in-one-word)

The internet in one word: **enshittification**. Every platform follows the same arc — first it serves its users, then its business customers, then only its shareholders — until what made it worth visiting is gone. One word captures the whole decay, from thriving town square to ad-choked husk. This entry prints exactly that word to stdout.
